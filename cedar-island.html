CEDAR ISLAND

OVERVIEW

The Artificial Cedar Island: Lebanon’s Global Anchor


The World’s Largest Man-Made Wonder & Economic Engine

Despite the rapid evolution of global real estate, the Mediterranean has long awaited a landmark that captures the true soul and pioneering spirit of Lebanon. While our nation’s leadership and people have always strived to globalize our civic and economic presence, the market has lacked a singular, "Brilliant Idea" to unify our diaspora and showcase our architectural superiority until now.


The Vision


The Artificial Cedar Island is not just a development; it is the world’s largest symbolic structure. Shaped as the iconic Lebanese Cedar, this project spans 3.3 million square meters of reclaimed land and features a staggering 4 million square meters of built-up area. It is a master-planned ecosystem designed to be the "World Property and Investment Center."


Project Highlights


  • A Mixed-Use Sanctuary: A seamless blend of ultra-luxury residential living, world-class commercial hubs, and vibrant community spaces.
  • The Global Marina & Amenities: A state-of-the-art international marina surrounded by lush landscaping and elite amenities that redefine Mediterranean luxury.
  • Technological Integration: Utilizing AR, AI, and Virtual Connectivity, the island will serve as a global exchange, allowing Lebanese investors and businesses from over 50 cities worldwide to collaborate in real-time.


Economic Impact & National Support


This project stands as a pillar of support for the Lebanese Government and a beacon for the Lebanese Diaspora. It is designed to bridge the gap between our citizens abroad and their homeland:


  • Job Creation: Generating tens of thousands of specialized jobs for Lebanese professionals.
  • Industrial Growth: Stimulating local manufacturing and supply chains to support the massive construction and operational phases.
  • Diaspora Gateway: Providing a secure, world-class investment vehicle for Lebanese living outside the country to reclaim their stake in Lebanon’s future.


A Global Hub


The Artificial Cedar Island will act as the International Real Estate Exchange Center, an economic hub where finance and business sectors converge. It is the answer to a world yearning for a centralized investment landmark, proving once and for all Lebanon’s status as a global pioneer.
