I LOVE BOSNIA – Breza

OVERVIEW

Luxury Wellness & Villa Resort Destination


Project Brief


I LOVE BOSNIA is envisioned as a landmark luxury wellness and hospitality destination located in Breza, Bosnia & Herzegovina, approximately 30–40 minutes from Sarajevo International Airport.

Inspired by the natural forests, mountains, and clean climate of Bosnia, the project combines:


  • Luxury villas
  • Wellness retreat facilities
  • Boutique hospitality
  • Nature tourism
  • Family entertainment
  • Eco-living experiences


The development aims to position Breza as:

“The luxury wellness capital of Bosnia for Global investors.


Project Vision


To create one of the first integrated luxury wellness resort communities in Bosnia that blends:

  • Alpine nature
  • Wellness living
  • Hospitality
  • Luxury real estate
  • Adventure tourism


The concept is designed to attract:

  • Global families
  • International investors
  • Wellness tourism operators
  • Holiday home buyers
  • Medical wellness travelers


Master Plan Overview


Based on the attached master plan, the development includes a diversified tourism and residential ecosystem across approximately 122,902 m² built-up area.

Main Components


Luxury Weekend Settlement


  • Approximately 120+ luxury holiday villas
  • Multiple villa typologies
  • Forest and mountain views
  • Private gardens and terraces


Hotel & Wellness District


  • Boutique luxury hotel
  • Wellness and spa center
  • Medical wellness potential
  • Central square and gathering areas


Hospitality & Commercial Zone


  • Restaurants
  • Cafés
  • Retail market
  • Outdoor dining areas
  • Viewing decks


Recreation & Adventure Facilities


  • Zipline
  • Horse riding center
  • Picnic zones
  • Walking trails
  • Sports facilities
  • Forest wellness paths


Residential & Serviced Apartments


  • Residential blocks
  • Serviced apartment concept
  • Long-stay wellness residences


Community & Lifestyle Facilities


  • Religious facility
  • Service buildings
  • Hospitality support facilities
  • Green public spaces


Strategic Location Advantage


✔ Near Sarajevo

  • Easy airport access
  • Strong tourism gateway


✔ Natural Environment

  • Forests
  • Mountains
  • Fresh air
  • Cooler climate compared to Gulf countries


✔ Affordable Luxury Destination

Bosnia offers:

  • Lower development cost
  • High luxury perception potential
  • Competitive pricing compared to Europe


Unique Selling Proposition (USP)


“Luxury Wellness Living in Nature”

The project is not positioned as a standard resort, but rather as:


  • A lifestyle destination
  • A wellness retreat
  • A luxury second-home community
  • A four-season tourism experience


Target Markets


Global Market


Key Drivers


  • Cool weather
  • Privacy
  • Nature escape
  • Family-friendly environment


European Market

  • Wellness travelers
  • Nature tourism
  • Long-stay retirees
  • Holiday home investors


Investment Positioning


The project targets premium positioning with luxury villa pricing potentially reaching:

USD 3,500/m²

This pricing strategy is supported through:

  • Wellness branding
  • Luxury architecture
  • Nature integration
  • Hospitality services
  • International-level resort standards


Architectural Direction


Style

  • Contemporary Alpine Luxury
  • Modern European resort architecture
  • Natural materials:
    • Stone
    • Wood
    • Glass


Features

  • Large panoramic windows
  • Infinity pools
  • Wellness terraces
  • Sauna & spa integration
  • Mountain-view outdoor spaces


Sustainability & Wellness


The development promotes:

  • Eco-tourism
  • Wellness living
  • Green mobility
  • Walkable communities
  • Forest integration
  • Healthy lifestyle experiences


Potential additions:

  • Longevity center
  • Medical wellness clinic
  • Organic farming
  • Detox programs
  • AI-powered wellness systems


Potential Branding Opportunities


The project may later integrate:

  • International wellness operators
  • Branded hospitality
  • Luxury resort partnerships
  • Wellness programs


Conclusion


I LOVE BOSNIA is designed to become:

A landmark luxury wellness and villa destination in Bosnia that combines hospitality, nature, wellness, and investment opportunities into one integrated resort ecosystem.

The project has the potential to become:


  • A flagship tourism destination
  • A luxury second-home market
  • A long-term investment platform in Bosnia & Herzegovina.
